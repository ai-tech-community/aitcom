# Join Us Post Series — Design Document

**Date:** 2026-03-01
**Project:** AIT Community — Recruitment Campaign
**Status:** Approved

## Goal

Attract students, vibe coders, junior developers, and senior developers to join AIT Community through a social media post series with images.

## Strategy

**Approach B — "Story + Invitation" (one narrative, four lenses)**

One shared core narrative across all posts: *"We're building a place where humans and AI build together — not a course, not a bootcamp, a living community. And we need every kind of builder."*

Each post frames that story through a different lens so each audience sees themselves in it.

## Platforms

- **LinkedIn** — longer copy, deeper hooks, professional reach
- **Twitter/X** — punchy copy, under 280 characters, viral potential

## Series Structure

| # | Audience | Lens | Hook |
|---|----------|------|------|
| 1 | Students | Learning | "You don't need experience. You need curiosity." |
| 2 | Vibe Coders | Creative freedom | "Build what feels right. Ship when it's alive." |
| 3 | Junior Devs | Growth | "The gap between junior and senior is smaller than you think." |
| 4 | Senior Devs | Legacy + mentorship | "The next generation needs to see how you think." |

**Series hashtag:** `#BuildTogether`

## Image Direction

**Style:** Human + warm — real people, natural light, collaborative energy. Not corporate, not stock-photo generic.

| Post | Image Concept |
|------|---------------|
| Students | Student at desk, focused, natural light, community feel |
| Vibe Coders | Multiple screens, side project energy, night or golden hour light |
| Junior Devs | Two people at laptops, one explaining to the other, mentorship feel |
| Senior Devs | Someone thinking deeply, whiteboard or notebook nearby, calm authority |

**Brand alignment:** Warm tones with subtle amber/orange accents. AIT logo + URL visible. Clean modern sans-serif typography for text overlays.

## CTAs (layered)

1. Visit `aitcommunity.org` — drives traffic
2. Engage in comments — sparks conversation, boosts algorithmic reach
3. DM directly — personal connection, founder filters who joins

## Deliverables

- [marketing/linkedin/join-us-series.md](../../marketing/linkedin/join-us-series.md) — LinkedIn copy for all 4 posts
- [marketing/x-twitter/join-us-series.md](../../marketing/x-twitter/join-us-series.md) — X/Twitter copy for all 4 posts
- 4 image assets (to be created — one per post, works for both platforms)

## Posting Plan

- Space posts 2-3 days apart
- Post at peak hours: 9-10am or 6-8pm local time
- Engage with every reply within the first hour
- Engage especially on posts 1 and 3 — highest expected engagement
- Pin post 4 (seniors) if strong replies come in — social proof from experienced devs builds trust for all audiences
